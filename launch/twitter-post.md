# Launch post

Random weekend project with Astra: Learn Your Physique.

I wanted to understand how developing different muscles changes our shape, so I built a 3D explorer.

Pick a muscle, move a slider, compare the physique.

Try it: https://atharva-create.github.io/learn-your-physique/

## Suggested first reply

236 muscle groups and parts, anatomy + physique views, and side-by-side comparisons.

It's an illustrative learning tool, not a prediction of your training results. The idea is to connect what you train with the muscles underneath.

Source: https://github.com/atharva-create/learn-your-physique
